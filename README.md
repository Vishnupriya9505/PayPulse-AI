# PayPulse AI 💳⚡

> Smart audit. Instant savings.
> An AI-powered subscription co-pilot that scans recurring spend, pinpoints hidden redundancies, and surfaces actionable monthly savings using Google Gemini and PayPal.

---

## 💡 Inspiration

Managing recurring digital subscriptions has become an overwhelming headache. Between streaming platforms, SaaS tools, and monthly memberships, most people lose track of active auto-renewals—silently burning hundreds of dollars annually on services they rarely use. We built **PayPulse AI** to act as an automated financial co-pilot: a tool that scans your subscriptions, pinpoints hidden redundancies, and surfaces actionable savings instantly.

---

## ✨ What It Does

PayPulse AI provides a clean, data-driven dashboard for tracking and optimizing subscription spend:
Interactive Spend Tracker:Uses AG Grid to display, sort, and organize monthly subscription costs in real time.
AI-Powered Financial Audits:Leverages Google's Gemini models to analyze spending patterns, flag duplicate services, and generate custom cost-cutting insights.
Premium Insights Access:Integrates the PayPal JavaScript SDK to deliver instant, secure transactions for unlocking advanced financial reports.

---
## What's next for PayPulse AI

Plaid API Integration:Connect live bank feeds to automatically import transactions instead of relying on manual subscription entry.
One-Click Cancellations:Partner with subscription management protocols to let users cancel unwanted services directly from the dashboard.
Predictive Renewal Alerts:Implement automated email/SMS reminders 48 hours before annual or monthly auto-renewals trigger.
